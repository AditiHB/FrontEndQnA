Our story

Bunny learned to bake in her grandmother's kitchen, standing on a wooden box to reach the counter. Her first loaf was a little flat, and everybody ate it anyway.

Today she gets up before the sun to start the ovens, so that the first warm bread is on the shelf when the neighbours walk by. Every recipe is still made the slow, careful way she was taught.
